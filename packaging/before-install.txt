Carl-Cam development build

Carl-Cam processes live camera images locally. It does not save photos or video.
MediaPipe may send performance and usage metrics to Google; its input images
and video are processed on this computer and are not sent to Google.
Personal gesture training saves numerical examples only when you opt in.
No trained gesture demo is bundled yet: choose personal training or mouse/keyboard.

The camera and optional virtual output are off when Carl-Cam opens. This installer
does not install OBS, camera drivers, Python or a CUDA toolkit. OBS Virtual Camera
is a separate prerequisite if you want outgoing video.

CPU masking is the default. The experimental NVIDIA masking component is bundled;
try it from Settings > Camera, save, then stop and restart the camera. It needs a
compatible NVIDIA GPU and driver. A missing or failed GPU path falls back to CPU.
Bundled runtime libraries do not require a separate CUDA toolkit installation.

Personal neural training also has a separate optional "Train on NVIDIA GPU"
choice when reviewing recordings. CPU training remains the default. GPU fitting
requires a compatible NVIDIA card and driver; failures are reported with a CPU
retry. Finished models remain CPU-loadable. This choice does not change the
masking processor, and GPU training is not guaranteed to be faster for small
gesture networks.

Close Carl-Cam before updating. Uninstall removes program files and shortcuts;
your local numerical recordings, personal models and settings remain. Use the
app's Settings to remove your recordings/models separately.

This is an unsigned development build, not an approved public release. Real-person
gesture checks, live camera/OBS tests, final graphics, performance tuning and
clean Windows testing remain required. No successful checks are assumed.
